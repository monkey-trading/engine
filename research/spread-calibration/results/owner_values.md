## The corrected income model

The model now charges each churn class `fee(h) * sigma * sqrt(h) * psi(2 s / (sigma * sqrt(h)))` over its holds, and positions held an hour or more the fee on their realised PnL less the spread (`research/cap-calibration/model/holdfee.py`). The flow records only each churn class's mean hold and mean square-root hold, so the lower bound takes the least fee any distribution of holds with those two moments allows (a linear programme on three holds); the central estimate tilts the measured within-hour holding profile to the same moments. Every account pays tier 3's fee (8% past the curve), the least any tier pays. #614's decision D5 is not modelled: every account trades at the market's own leverage, as tiers 2 and 3 do, while one below tier 2 would pay a higher fee on less notional where the market allows more than 20x (tier 0) or 50x (tier 1). The flow data does not say how volume spreads across tiers.

Spreads are each market's first tier on both fills: D6's tenths on FX and indices, #623's whole bps elsewhere. Larger fills would pay more, so spread income is also understated.
