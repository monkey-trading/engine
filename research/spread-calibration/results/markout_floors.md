Generated by `python -I run.py floors`. The floor is the year's markout spread (#614, 2026-11-13), the root mean square of the index's 5 s changes over the open hours: measured on the trade grids of BTC-USD, ETH-USD and SOL-USD, and scaled from the hourly bars elsewhere by the crypto markets' ratio of measured to Brownian-scaled markout spread, 0.869 (BTC-USD 0.916, ETH-USD 0.904, SOL-USD 0.787). Honest accounts' markouts are the crypto hours' random fills in their hour's units (standard deviation 1.00, kurtosis 13). Reach is the share of 4,000 edgeless accounts whose score reaches tier 1 (a score of 4) or tier 2 (8) within 1,000 fills: trailing, each markout in its hour's units or the floor's when the hour is quieter; at the floor, every markout in the floor's units, the most holding the index still can do.
