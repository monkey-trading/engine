# Resting-order limits by volume tier (#700)

Generated by `python -I run.py order_limits`. What each limit is worth against wallet recycling, and what resting orders cost a price.

## Worth: what recycling forfeits

The latency strategy at the owner's fee (100% to 1 s, 10% at 15 minutes), after the spreads, the size tiers and the stick, on an operator-sized position: the better of keeping a wallet and recycling one at tier 0, and at the recycler's best what it gains (the stick's add-on) and forfeits (the kept tier's best-price size and leverage, and the wallets), each as the account's profit a day. It trades with market orders, so moving to tier 0's 25 resting orders forfeits nothing.

## Cost: what resting orders add to a price

`crates/node/src/sequencer/resting_bench.rs`: a 100k-account sequencer with market 1's first spread band at 2 bps and that many limit buys inside it, which every price reaches and the spread keeps out of reach, so each stays resting and is priced again on every price. The price command's stamp, which every later price waits behind, against `price_wait_budget`'s 1 ms median. Run on a shared machine under load.

| Out-of-reach resting orders | Samples | Stamp p50 | p99 | Worst |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 200 | 0.12 ms | 5.50 ms | 20.09 ms |
| 1,000 | 200 | 0.65 ms | 0.92 ms | 0.94 ms |
| 10,000 | 200 | 5.16 ms | 9.52 ms | 20.25 ms |
| 100,000 | 200 | 52.19 ms | 120.23 ms | 120.32 ms |
| 1,000,000 | 20 | 624.43 ms | 940.78 ms | 940.78 ms |
| 2,500,000 | 20 | 1766.20 ms | 2542.25 ms | 2542.25 ms |

A price costs 115 us and 0.58 us more for each such order, so about 1,531 of them in one market take a price's own stamp to the median budget.

With 2,500,000 resting limits away from the price, every account at tier 0's limit, which no price reaches, a price's stamp is 0.11 ms at the median and 0.28 ms at the 99th percentile: the index skips orders a price has not reached.
